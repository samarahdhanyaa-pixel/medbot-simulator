Aluminium chassis, two DC geared motors with encoders, closed 10 kg tray, battery and motor driver.

Kinematics
FKv = r(ωR+ωL)/2   ω = r(ωR−ωL)/L
IKvR = v + ωL/2   vL = v − ωL/2
POSEẋ = v cos θ   ẏ = v sin θ   θ̇ = ω

Non-holonomic: ẋ sin θ − ẏ cos θ = 0, so the robot turns on the spot when the path is behind it.

Dynamics · Euler–Lagrange
T = ½mv² + ½Ibω² + ½Iw(φ̇R²+φ̇L²)
Meff v̇ = (τR+τL)/r − Frr
Ieff ω̇ = (τR−τL)L / 2r

Meff = m + 2Iw/r², Frr = 0.02·m·g.

Simulation loop
Hospital map→Inflate walls 0.3 m→A* (8-neighbour)→Resample 5 cm→Pure Pursuit / PID→v, ω and accel. limits→IK + wheel limit→FK + Euler pose→Inverse dynamics τ↺ every 0.05 s
